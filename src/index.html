How to Play

Click the SPIN button to spin the slot machine!

🎰 Game Grid

The game displays a 6-row by 5-column grid of symbols:

  • PRISONER 👤
  • ROBBER 💰
  • COP 👮
  • BAR
  • 7
  • CHERRY 🍒
  • BELL 🔔

🚨 Bonus Trigger

The bonus is triggered when ALL of the following conditions are met in any row:

  1. PRISONER appears in the leftmost column (column 1)
  2. ROBBER appears in the rightmost column (column 5)
  3. COP appears in any middle column (columns 2, 3, or 4)

When the bonus triggers, the symbols will be highlighted and you'll hear a siren! 🚨
